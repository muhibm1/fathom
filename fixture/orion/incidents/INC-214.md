## What happened

A change to `jobs/ingest_events.py` switched the partition key of `raw_events` from
`event_date` to `ingest_ts` to speed up loads. Late-arriving events landed in the wrong
day, `stg_events` double-counted the overlap, and `fct_daily_active_users` reported a
40% spike that paged product analytics. Rolling back required a full rewrite of the
table (six hours).

## Why it was not caught

The change looked local to one Spark job. Nothing told the author that four models and
a dashboard read `event_date` as a partition filter.

## Decision

`raw_events` is partitioned by `event_date`, never by `ingest_ts`. Recorded as CON-014.
