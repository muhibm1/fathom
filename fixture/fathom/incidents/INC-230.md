## What happened

A cleanup PR removed `discount_pct` from `fct_orders` because "nothing uses it". `net_amount`
in `fct_revenue` is computed from it, and `dash_revenue_weekly` showed gross instead of net
revenue for one afternoon. Finance noticed before the Monday review.

## Decision

Columns of `fct_revenue` and the `fct_orders` columns it derives from are a public contract.
Removals need finance-data sign-off. Recorded as CON-031.
