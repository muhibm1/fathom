## What happened

A two-week backfill of `raw_events` was started at 13:00 UTC from the Airflow UI. It took
the shared `spark_pool`, `orders_pipeline` missed three hourly SLAs, and `fct_orders` was
stale for finance for the afternoon.

## Decision

No backfills on `raw_events` between 06:00 and 20:00 UTC. Recorded as CON-022.
